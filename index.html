03 A page from the book

Embalming: what it is, and when it isn't required.

The truth

No federal law requires embalming for every death. Funeral homes may ask for it when you choose a public viewing, but they may not tell you it is required by law when it is not.

What really happens

Embalming replaces the body's fluids with a preserving solution so that the person can be viewed with dignity over several days. Refrigeration is the usual alternative when there is no viewing, or when the service is soon.

What to ask

“Is embalming required for the arrangements I'm choosing? Please show me where that is written. And what does refrigeration cost instead?”
