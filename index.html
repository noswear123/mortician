Your rights

The federal Funeral Rule says you can:

  • Get prices by phone, and an itemized price list in person
  • Pick only the goods and services you want
  • Buy a casket elsewhere, with no fee for bringing it
  • Decline embalming unless a law or a specific arrangement requires it

Most families never hear this at the arrangement table. The e-book shows you how to use it, politely and firmly.

05 Questions

Before you decide.

Is this only for someone who has just lost a loved one?

No. Many readers are planning ahead for themselves or a parent, which is the best time to read it, because nobody is rushed. It is also useful in the first days after a death, when decisions are due quickly.

Is this against funeral directors?

No. Samuel was one for over forty years, and he respects the work. The e-book is about informed choices. Many funeral homes are honest and caring; the aim is that you can tell the difference and ask the right questions.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you are taken to a download page for the PDF, and we also email you a link you can use again later. It opens on any phone, tablet or computer, and prints cleanly on ordinary paper.

Are the dollar figures exact?

No. They are U.S. planning ranges, not quotes. Funeral prices vary a great deal by state, city and provider, which is exactly why the e-book teaches you how to get the real numbers near you.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is education from long experience. Funeral, burial and cremation rules differ by state, so check the rules where you live, and speak to an attorney or adviser for anything that needs one.
